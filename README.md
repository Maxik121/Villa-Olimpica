# 🏛️ Villa Olympica

> Scan a label at the Getty Villa and your family becomes Olympic athletes in Roblox, rescuing Hermes by playing contests built around the real objects in the galleries.

**Villa Olympica** is a Roblox game that turns a visit to the [J. Paul Getty Museum's Getty Villa](https://www.getty.edu/visit/villa) into a family scavenger hunt. It was built by team **ZIntensive Imagination** for the **Getty Villa Challenge** hackathon.

## The idea

### The problem

The Getty Villa is a free museum of the ancient world, and most of its visitors are locals. Its family activities today are on paper or audio (Art Detective trivia cards, the Percy Jackson–inspired "Demigods" quest, an Odyssey tour, the Family Forum) – there is no game. With Los Angeles hosting the 2028 Games, the Villa wants families to link its antiquities to the Olympics: on site, easy to finish, with a reward.

### Our player

The whole family on **one phone**: the child plays, the grown-up reads the label. Families already come to the Villa – kids who live on Roblox are the part the museum misses.

### The story

Every four years, wars stopped for the Games. Jealous **Hades** kidnapped **Hermes**, the Custodian of the Games, and locked him in the underworld. **Zeus needs a champion.**

You play an athlete of the ancient Games and pick a guide:

- **Hercules** – action contests
- **Athena** – puzzles at the objects

Collect leaves to restore the victor's wreath and break Hades' seal.

## Core loop

**Find it. Look at it. Play it. Keep it.**

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Find** | Follow glowing leaves to a gallery and scan the QR code on the object's label. |
| **2. Look** | Spot a detail on the real object while the grown-up reads the label. |
| **3. Play** | A 90-second contest of the event painted on the object. |
| **4. Collect** | Leaves, a sprig of the wreath, upgrades, a place on the leaderboard. |

### The first 10 minutes

| Time | Moment |
|------|--------|
| 0:00 | Scan the first label. Zeus waits at the door. |
| 0:30 | Hades has taken Hermes. Win the Games to free him. |
| 1:30 | Pick a guide: Hercules or Athena. |
| 2:30 | The game copy stays veiled until you scan the real jar in Gallery 104. |
| 5:00 | Chariot race against Apollo, like the one painted on the jar. |
| 6:30 | Win: a sprig for the wreath and a shower of leaves. |
| 8:00 | Spend leaves on a cape, a flame, a pet. |
| 9:30 | Your time on the Hall of Victors. Next object, next gallery. |

## The visit

**Played during the visit. Kept going after it.**

- **During (25–40 minutes):** contests unlock by scanning at the object. The game points, the museum shows. Winners get a victor certificate on screen; we propose an olive-leaf sticker at the info desk.
- **After, at home:** replay contests to beat your records, upgrade your athlete and climb the leaderboard.
- **Why come back:** weekly and all-time leaderboards today. Next: new collectibles to restore each season, challenges with friends and family, and challenges that change with each exhibition, building up to LA28.

## Learning and impact

Most museum visitors spend well under a minute with an artwork (Smith, Smith & Tinio, 2017). In Villa Olympica every reward depends on looking.

**What the player learns**

- The olive wreath was the only Olympic prize.
- Prize jars held Athena's olive oil.
- The five pentathlon events.
- Athletes oiled and scraped their skin.
- Herakles founded the Games in myth.

**What the museum can measure**

- Logged today: the same question at the start and in the final riddle, first-try answers about each real object, and progress from first scan to crown.
- Next: return visits, from the scan dates we already save.

## Safe and easy

**Scan, tap, play. Nothing to type, nothing to buy.**

- **First-time player:** the QR opens Roblox straight in the game and unlocks that object. Picture answers, one big button, sound off in the galleries.
- **Chat:** switched off in the game (Roblox also keeps chat off by default for accounts aged 5–8).
- **Spending:** only bay leaves earned by playing and scanning. No Robux, no ads, no loot boxes.
- **Age:** family mode for all ages – picture answers, a grown-up corner, time attack for teens. Built for a **Minimal** content maturity rating, which can reach Roblox Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15).

## What we built during the hackathon

We rebuilt the **whole Getty Villa in Roblox** – its rooms, part of the collection and the terrain – and implemented the game on top of it.

## How we got here

We started with a wall of ideas: scavenger hunt, storytelling through artefacts, PvP duels, brainrot "steal" games, AR or mixed reality, live events for 2028. The hunt with a story won the most votes – it fits the Villa's paper hunts, works with a QR code on a label and gives every object a story.

Feedback from Broken Egg and Xolbor shaped the scope:

| Feedback | Our response |
|----------|--------------|
| "Hard to fit in a manageable project" | Launch with two contests |
| "Connect to the museum's identity" | Each contest is the event painted on a real object |
| "Be aware of time" | 90-second contests |

What we kept from the Villa's paper quests:

| Paper quest | In the game |
|-------------|-------------|
| The card held at an object | A QR label |
| Its "look closely" question | A picture question about the real object |
| The grown-up reading aloud | The grown-up corner |
| The finish at the desk | A victor certificate |
